# Content guide

Use only realtor-approved listings and rights-cleared photos. Give each photo accurate alt text. Avoid publishing exact residential addresses, tenant names, security details, title documents, or personal data. Use the broadest accurate public location.

State the asking price and period explicitly. For each required fee, choose a known amount, not applicable, or unknown; do not guess a total. Confirm availability, furnishing, service-charge coverage, vehicle condition, import status, and document claims with the realtor before publication.

Keep safety guidance practical and neutral: arrange viewings through the realtor, inspect property and vehicle documents independently, and do not send deposits before verification. The platform does not independently verify claims.
